# KYC and KYB Requirements for Card Issuance

A guide to KYC and KYB requirements for card issuance, ensuring compliance, security, and seamless onboarding.

To ensure a secure and compliant experience, we have implemented Know Your Customer (KYC) and Know Your Business (KYB) requirements. These regulatory processes help verify the identity of individuals and businesses applying for a card, ensuring compliance with financial regulations while preventing fraud and illicit activities.

This guide outlines the essential information needed to meet these requirements and successfully onboard cardholders.

# Understanding KYC and KYB

## What is KYC/KYB?

KYC (Know Your Customer) and KYB (Know Your Business) are verification processes used by financial institutions to authenticate customers and businesses. These checks help prevent identity fraud, financial crimes, and unauthorized transactions.

* **KYC**: Applies to individuals and involves collecting personal information such as name, date of birth, and government-issued identification details.
* **KYB**: Applies to businesses and requires verification of entity details, such as business registration, ownership structure, and operational addresses.

## Why is KYC/KYB Important?

* **Regulatory Compliance**: Ensures adherence to local and international financial regulations.
* **Fraud Prevention**: Verifies card applicants to mitigate identity theft and fraudulent activity.
* **Security & Risk Management**: Protects both businesses and consumers from financial risks and unauthorized transactions.
* **Smooth Transaction Experience**: Enables secure card usage by ensuring that only legitimate customers and businesses can access financial services.

# KYC Requirements

If your customer is an individual, you must provide KYC information to Reap during the card creation process. The required details include:

* Full Name: The cardholder's legal name.
* Date of Birth (DOB): The cardholder's birthdate.
* Registered Address: The cardholder’s residential address.
* Government ID Information:
  * ID Type: Passport, national ID, or driver’s license.
  * ID Number: The unique identifier issued by the government.

These details are essential to verify the identity and legitimacy of each cardholder, ensuring regulatory compliance and risk mitigation.

# KYB Requirements

If your customer represents a business or corporation, you must provide KYB information to Reap during the card creation process. The required details include:

* Business Name: The official registered name of the business entity.
* Business Registration Number: A unique identifier assigned during business registration.
* Registered Address: The legal address of the business entity.

These requirements help confirm the legitimacy of your customer's business and ensure compliance with financial regulations.

# Seamless and Asynchronous Verification

To enhance efficiency, our KYC/KYB verification processes run asynchronously. This allows you to proceed with the card creation process while verification occurs in the background, reducing delays and improving the onboarding experience.

# Collaborative Compliance and Risk Management

In cases where suspicious activity is detected, we will engage in a collaborative resolution process with your company. Our team will work with you on a case-by-case basis to address and resolve any concerns regarding customer verification or fraudulent transactions.

By adhering to KYC/KYB requirements, you help ensure a secure, compliant, and frictionless card issuance process.
