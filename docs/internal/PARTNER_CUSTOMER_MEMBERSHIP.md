# Partner customer service policy

Customers acquired through authenticated API or white-label onboarding are attributed by `api_tenant_end_users` and immutable `account_origin_provenance`, not email domains, country, partner operator identity or editable Auth metadata. Existing signup writes this membership. No new table competing with that identity is introduced. Sandbox resources stay separate; no live customer membership is manufactured for test resources.

`partner_customer_directory` provides the internal customer ID, tenant ID, partner company name, external user reference and onboarding channel. It is service-role only. `partner_customer_memberships(uuid[])` offers bounded lookups for existing admin integrations. Membership remains effective if the partner is inactive; it does not enable any API, financial access or provider capability.

Partner customers are excluded from direct BorderPay broadcast recipient lists. Explicit stale selections are rejected before sending; the email sender separately checks both customer ID and recipient email. Non-essential direct emails (welcome, marketing, maintenance and automated status notices) are suppressed. Email verification, password reset and PIN recovery continue through the existing authenticated email and white-label branding paths. Authentication emails generated by Supabase Auth remain unchanged. No suppression is reported as sent.

Maintenance eligibility, monthly subscription creation, external invoice queueing, internal/VA/wallet maintenance collection, grace restrictions and provider restriction job execution exclude partner customers. Existing general security/compliance freezes and partner commercial pricing are not bypassed. The migration changes function definitions and adds service-only policy lookups; it does not execute billing, alter ledger balances, delete invoices, or enroll existing direct customers as partner customers. Any legacy membership transfer needs a separately reviewed migration and reconciliation.
