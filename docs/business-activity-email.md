# Business activity guidance

Business confirmation, founder welcome, day-one onboarding and verification reminder emails share accurate volume and cross-border guidance. A separate business_activity_update campaign is an account-service notice available across business verification statuses; it never changes account permissions or verification answers. Individual, demo, administrative and partner-managed accounts are excluded. Existing security bans and provider decisions are not changed.

The requested onboarding minimum is US$100,000+ monthly. The annual criterion is provisionally US$1 million or more pending clarification of an invalid requested range (US$1 million to US$999,999). Do not deploy or send before resolving this wording. These are BorderPay commercial onboarding criteria, not universal banking thresholds. Require truthful, supported forecasts; never direct merchants to inflate answers. No automatic hold or approval is promised. Merchants request a secure update link from support; the email does not mint a shared provider URL.

Send only through admin-email-ops, business_activity_update, in batches of at most 30, with a verified daily Brevo quota and transactional reserve. Fixed per-user idempotency keys prevent repeat sends of this version. Reconcile email_log statuses after each batch and stop on errors. All-status service notices retain individual/partner exclusions. Admin preview copy is byte-identical to the sender copy. Deployment changes only send-email and admin-email-ops, preserving their downloaded live dependencies; no mobile build, balances, payment state or provider verification is changed.

Tests cover original campaign eligibility, consumer/demo/partner rejection, all business states, quota failure, maximum batch size, stable idempotency, escaped merchant names and intact verification links. Vercel credentials currently return 403; do not claim the admin selector is published until verified on its correct project.
